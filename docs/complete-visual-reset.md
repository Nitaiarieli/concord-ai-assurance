# Concord complete visual reset

## Product definition

Concord helps enterprise security and AI-platform teams keep registered AI-derived state aligned with authoritative changes by tracing affected derivatives, executing bounded repair, and verifying actual retrieval behavior.

Primary audience: enterprise security, AI-platform, governance, and application owners evaluating a controlled assurance deployment.

Primary conversion: start a conversation about one registered control loop. Secondary conversion: open the authenticated workspace and connect the first eligible production application with a $0 application fee.

## Functionality preserved

- Public routes: product home, coverage, deterministic engine, pricing, Value & FinOps, and market intelligence.
- Authenticated routes: workspace, connector control center, and deployment architecture agent.
- Existing APIs, authentication boundaries, tenant isolation, data contracts, database bindings, pricing locks, simulations, contact behavior, tests, and hosting configuration.
- Product truth: registered and supported scope only; attempted remediation is not proof; destination read-back and behavioral retrieval testing are required; unresolved, unsupported, and accepted-risk states remain explicit.

## Creative directions considered

### 1. Verification Chamber — selected

An architectural assurance instrument. An authoritative change passes through five controlled apertures—Detect, Trace, Repair, Verify, Prove—and closes as a bounded evidence record. The composition combines severe black instrumentation, warm working paper, cobalt verification space, safety orange, and an acid confirmation signal.

Strengths: clearest five-second product explanation, highly ownable visual device, strong enterprise precision, direct mapping to the workflow, robust CSS implementation, and clean mobile reflow.

### 2. Revision Field

An editorial material system in which one source revision propagates through stacked registered layers. The experience would emphasize annotations, redlines, cuts, and corrected fragments.

Strengths: strong relationship to authoritative information and excellent copy-led storytelling. Tradeoff: it communicates information change better than permission change, behavioral verification, or destination read-back.

### 3. Signal Relay

A dark spatial relay that spotlights one active handoff at a time from authority to retrieval proof. The scene would use sharp light, long technical distances, and isolated system endpoints.

Strengths: cinematic and dramatic. Tradeoff: greater risk of becoming a generic cybersecurity network, requiring heavier rendering, and losing clarity on mobile.

## Design system

- Palette: working paper `#f2f0e9`, carbon `#121310`, deep moss `#244f43`, mineral rust `#c95b3b`, sand `#eed9b5`, and acid confirmation `#dce88f`, plus explicit state colors.
- Typography: Aptos/Segoe variable display and text stacks with large, open product claims and 18-24px primary reading copy; Cascadia Mono notation for evidence, timestamps, and instrument labels.
- Grid: 1480px maximum canvas, responsive gutters, visible 44px technical grid in the hero, hard alignment lines, and chapter-specific compositions.
- Shape language: square chambers, diamond apertures, direct line routes, circular state marks, no generic rounded-card system.
- Motion: short stage transitions, aperture alignment, inspector entry, progressive root lineage, and restrained fauna travel tied directly to scroll. No scroll hijacking, decorative particles, or autonomous floating.
- Interaction: roving-focus five-stage tabs, explicit playback, status text plus symbols, evidence filters, native mobile navigation disclosure, focus-managed object inspector, and contact dialog.
- Responsive behavior: split precision instrument on desktop; independent vertical choreography, horizontal stage rail, mobile bottom sheet, and stacked evidence/coverage surfaces on mobile.

## Living ecosystem refinement

- Background: an original generated 1718x920 panorama blends eroded desert terrain into humid rainforest through exposed soil and roots. The delivery JPEG is 274 KB and is reused with section-specific tonal overlays.
- Lineage: a seven-path, code-native SVG root system spans the landing page. Scroll progress reveals it from 14% to 100%, making registered data lineage visible across documents and applications.
- Fauna: original bird, oryx, and gecko silhouettes provide restrained spatial wayfinding. They are `aria-hidden`, use no pointer events, move only with scroll, and become static under reduced motion. The oryx is removed on narrow mobile screens to protect reading space.
- Typography: the system now uses a refined Aptos/Segoe variable stack with larger 18-24px reading copy, more open headline tracking and leading, and Cascadia Mono for evidence notation.
- Palette: moss, rust, sand, leaf, carbon, paper, and acid confirmation colors connect the technical product surfaces to the hybrid environment without turning the interface into a nature template.

The original background was created with the built-in image generation workflow for this project, then copied into `public/concord-ecosystem-v2.jpg` and optimized from 2.43 MB PNG to a 274 KB JPEG. The generation requested a physically plausible desert-to-rainforest transition, calm negative space for interface overlays, subtle exposed roots, no people, no technology, no animals, no text, and no copied brand imagery.

## Research use

Oak informed the balance of concise brand statement, concrete product proof, and enterprise conversion. All chosen behaviors were implemented natively in the Concord system; no catalog component was installed.
