# Concord design constraints

These constraints carry forward across future Concord iterations in this conversation unless the user explicitly changes them.

## Product truth

- Concord is an independent assurance and reconciliation plane for registered AI-derived state.
- The core path is: authoritative change → registered impact → policy-specific repair → destination read-back → identity-aware retrieval test → evidence.
- Attempted remediation is not successful completion.
- A successful write or API response is not proof.
- Unsupported, repairing, unresolved, and accepted-risk states remain distinct from verified.
- The current MVP boundary is SharePoint and Entra as authorities, with Pinecone and Redis as downstream derivatives and a registered retrieval proof path.
- Do not imply universal discovery, immediate consistency, inline enforcement, or coverage outside supported adapters.
- The current launch posture remains design-partner staging only.

## Landing-page story

- Use approximately five visible chapters.
- Explain the product within five seconds.
- Every chapter must answer what changed, what is affected, what can be repaired, what can be proven, or why it matters.
- Move secondary detail into objects, tabs, disclosures, drawers, or modals.
- Use one primary focal point per chapter and one primary action at the end.

## Visual system

- The earlier Concord site remains the visual foundation; current content and behavior remain authoritative.
- Use a warm, premium, cinematic forest–desert world with dark olive, charcoal, warm gold, cream, and controlled lime.
- Preserve editorial typography, architectural grids, technical labels, coordinates, and numbered chapters.
- Natural environments must explain source truth, lineage, invalid state, remediation, and verified coverage.
- Prefer realistic materials, restrained motion, generous spacing, and readable type.
- Avoid generic SaaS layouts, repetitive rounded cards, glassmorphism, bright gradients, decorative shapes, and visual overload.

## Motion, access, and performance

- Every major chapter receives one meaningful motion transformation.
- Motion supports comprehension and never blocks reading or navigation.
- Mobile receives simplified compositions rather than compressed desktop scenes.
- Preserve keyboard behavior, visible focus, semantic structure, touch targets, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
- Reuse optimized local imagery and code-native diagrams; avoid unnecessary heavy media.
